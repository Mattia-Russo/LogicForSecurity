@startuml PhotoPrint_W5
title PhotoPrint — Week 5\n(TLS pseudonymous channels replace explicit encryption)

participant "A\n(User)" as A
participant "IdP\n(Identity Provider)" as IDP
participant "P\n(Photo Service)" as P
participant "B\n(Storage Server)" as B

note over A : A obtains pk(P) and pk(B) \n via KeyLookUp_W5

activate A

A -> IDP : [A] *->* IdP\nf_auth(A, P, B, pwd(A), Na)
note right: TLS channel to IdP \n replaces {f_auth(...)}pk(idp) \n pwd(A) protected by TLS
activate IDP

IDP --> A : {f_grant(A, P, B, Grant, Na)}inv(pk(idp))
note right: Signature kept: \n transferable credential \n for P and B to verify
deactivate IDP

A -> P : [A] *->* P\n{f_grant(A, P, B, Grant, Na)}inv(pk(idp)) 
note right: TLS channel to P \n replaces outer pk(P) encryption \n inner signature kept for P to verify
activate P

P -> B : [P] *->* B\nf_req({f_grant(A,P,B,Grant,Na)}inv(pk(idp)), Np)
note right: TLS channel to B \n replaces pk(B) encryption \n inner signature kept for B to verify
activate B

B --> P : B *->* [P] \n f_photos(photos(A), Np)
note right: TLS reply direction \n replaces {f_photos(...)}pk(P)
deactivate B

deactivate P
deactivate A

@enduml
